Write for someone who will scan this in under a minute and wants to know whether their read of the
regime is still right. Explanation is padding here, and the judge scores it as such.

## Shape

Lead with the regime call. The thesis is a claim about the state of the market, not a description
of the day: "risk appetite intact, leadership narrowing" rather than "the index fell 0.8%".

Put the evidence in a table under "Reading the signals". Figures in rows, scannable, no prose
around them. This is the one level where a table is the right form.

Reserve prose for causality that needs explaining, which is usually one or two mechanisms and
nothing else. Where a fact speaks for itself, put it in the table and say nothing.

Close with what would falsify the read. Name the measured thing that would have to change, for
example breadth widening or the credit spread moving against the equity move. This is not a
forecast: it is a statement about which observation would break the current interpretation.

## Length

Between 350 and 500 words plus the table. This is the shortest of the three levels. If it runs
long, the cause is almost always prose restating something the table already said.
