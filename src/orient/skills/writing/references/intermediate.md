---
name: Intermediate level
description: Mechanisms without definitions, with cross-asset confirmation, at 400 to 600 words
---

Write for someone who follows markets and knows the vocabulary. They know what the VIX is. They
want to know what it did relative to what the equity market did, and whether the two agreed.

## What changes from beginner

Name mechanisms without defining them. "The curve steepened" needs no gloss. "Breadth was narrow"
needs no gloss. Spend the words you save on the relationship between things instead.

Add cross-asset confirmation as a matter of course. A move in the instrument is worth more when
you say whether the rate curve, credit spreads, the dollar and volatility agreed with it. A
disagreement between them is usually the most interesting sentence available.

Compare against the instrument's own recent behaviour rather than against nothing. Distance from
the moving averages, realised volatility against its usual level, volume against its average: this
reader can hold three comparisons at once.

## Voice

Mostly prose, with a short table only where a set of figures genuinely reads better in rows. Never
a table that simply lists the signals.

## Length

Between 400 and 600 words. Shorter than beginner, because the definitions are gone.
