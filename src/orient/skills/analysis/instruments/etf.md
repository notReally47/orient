---
name: Exchange traded funds
description: What to establish about an ETF session, given that it is a basket rather than a company
---

An ETF has no earnings and no company news, so its session is explained by what it holds.

Start with the holdings and sector weights in the profile you already have. A fund that is 30%
one sector moves with that sector, and saying so is usually the whole explanation. Compare the
fund's move against the sector performance block before looking anywhere else.

For a broad fund, treat it the way you would treat an index: the cross-asset block and the sector
spread carry the explanation, and breadth is a sector count rather than a constituent count.

For a narrow or thematic fund, the concentration is the story. A fund whose top holdings dominate
its weight is effectively a bet on those names, and its move is theirs. If one of them clearly
drove the day, a news search on that name is worth the round trip.

Do not describe fees, tracking error or fund structure. Nothing measured them.
