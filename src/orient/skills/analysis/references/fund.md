A mutual fund prices once a day, so its bar is a net asset value and not a traded range.

There is no intraday move, no high, no low and no gap. Those measurements are absent rather than
zero, and the summary must not describe the fund as opening, trading through a range or closing.
Volume is absent for the same reason.

Its session is explained by what it holds. Use the holdings and sector weights on the profile and
set the move against the sector board: a fund concentrated in one sector moved with that sector,
and saying so is usually the entire explanation. `relative` measures it against the broad market
and carries no sector peer.

The cross-asset block still sets the backdrop, and a fund that moved against its sector on a day
the curve or the dollar moved sharply is worth pointing at.

Do not write about fees, loads, manager changes or flows. Nothing measured them.
