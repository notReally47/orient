---
name: Funds
description: What to establish about a mutual fund session, where pricing is daily rather than continuous
---

A mutual fund prices once a day, so its bar is a net asset value rather than a traded range. Do
not describe an intraday move, a high or a low as though the fund traded through them.

Its session is explained by what it holds. Use the holdings and sector weights in the profile, and
compare the move against the sector performance block. A fund concentrated in one sector moved
with that sector, and saying so is usually the entire explanation.

Because the price is struck once, volume against average means little here. Leave it out unless
the profile made it meaningful.

The cross-asset block still sets the backdrop, and a fund that moved against its sector on a day
the rate curve or the dollar moved sharply is worth pointing at.

Do not describe fees, loads, manager changes or flows. Nothing measured them.
