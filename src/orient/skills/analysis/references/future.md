---
name: Futures
description: What to establish about a futures session, and the contract mechanics not to invent
---

A futures contract is a claim on something physical or financial at a date, so its session is
about the underlying and about positioning, and you can only speak to the first.

Compare the move against the cross-asset block, which already holds crude and gold, the dollar
index and the rate curve. A commodity contract moving against a dollar move is often the whole
story, and the direction matters: a stronger dollar is a headwind for anything priced in dollars.

Realised volatility and volume against average carry more weight here than for an equity, because
a futures market with volume far above average and a large move is a market where positioning
changed, and that is a fact rather than a guess about who did what.

Do not describe the term structure, roll yield, open interest or the basis. None of them were
measured, and each reads precise enough that a reader would believe it.

Do not name the delivery month or contract size unless the profile supplied it.
