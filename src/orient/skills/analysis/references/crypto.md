---
name: Crypto
description: What to establish about a crypto session, where the usual reference points do not apply
---

Crypto trades continuously and has no earnings, no sector and no fundamental anchor in the data
you hold, so most of the usual comparisons are unavailable. Say less rather than reaching.

The comparisons that do work: the move against the equity risk backdrop in the cross-asset block,
since crypto and equity risk appetite move together often enough that agreement or disagreement on
a given day is worth naming, and the move against its own recent volatility, which is the most
honest way to say whether the day was large.

Because the session runs continuously, a daily bar is a slice rather than a session, and the
close is an arbitrary point in it. Do not describe an open or a close as though a bell rang.

Drawdown from the 52 week high is more informative here than almost anywhere else, because the
ranges are wide enough that a large daily move can still leave the instrument far from its high.

Do not describe on-chain activity, supply, halving schedules, exchange flows or adoption. Nothing
here measured any of them.
