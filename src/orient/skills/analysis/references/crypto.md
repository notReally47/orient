Crypto trades continuously and has no earnings, no sector and no fundamental anchor in the data
you hold. Say less rather than reaching.

Two comparisons work. The move against the equity risk backdrop in the cross-asset block, since
the two move together often enough that agreement or disagreement on a given day is worth naming;
and the move against its own recent volatility, which is the honest way to say whether the day was
large. `relative` sets an altcoin against Bitcoin, the one benchmark that means anything here, and
is absent for Bitcoin itself.

The session runs without a bell, so a daily bar is a slice and its close is an arbitrary point in
one. Do not describe an open or a close as though trading stopped.

Drawdown from the 52 week high is more informative here than almost anywhere, because the ranges
are wide enough that a large day can still leave the instrument far from its high.

Do not write about on-chain activity, supply, halving schedules, exchange flows or adoption.
Nothing measured them.
